What is REFUEL?

REFUEL is a smart fuel and charging station navigation platform designed for Indian roads. It helps users locate nearby petrol, diesel, CNG, and EV stations using real-time GPS and location intelligence.

Unlike traditional maps, REFUEL integrates crowd estimation, station availability, and predictive insights to guide users toward the fastest and most efficient fueling option — saving both time and fuel.

The Problem: Systemic Congestion

Unpredictable demand at fuel stations leads to long queues, wasted fuel, and traffic congestion. Current navigation systems only show location — they do not consider waiting time, crowd levels, or fuel availability, leading to inefficient decisions.
